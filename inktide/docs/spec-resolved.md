# INKTIDE — Rise of the Murk · Resolved Spec

This is the spec the game is built against. The request (in Turkish) was:

> A Splatoon-like game on three.js. Mechanics and look should match the genre one-to-one, single
> player, not a demo but a complete game with a story from start to finish, polished graphics,
> animations and effects. Full gamepad, mouse and keyboard support. Language: English.

Everything the request did not pin down is resolved below as `ASSUMPTION-nn`.

## Originality

- **ASSUMPTION-01 — Original IP, genre-faithful mechanics.** Game mechanics (ink turf, kid↔squid
  swim form, climbing inked walls, ink tank, sub/special weapons, turf-war scoring) are genre
  conventions and are reproduced faithfully. Names, characters, story, world, logos, music and art
  are **original**. Nothing from Nintendo's Splatoon IP is used (no Inklings/Octolings, no
  Inkopolis, no Squid Sisters, no Judd, no Octarians, no zapfish, no names of real weapons).
- **ASSUMPTION-02 — Procedural art.** The team's asset library (`D:\2026\AssetLibrary`) is not
  reachable from the cloud build container, and Higgsfield had 0.65 credits (a key-art image
  costs 2). Characters, enemies, bosses, props, textures, sky, music and SFX are therefore
  generated by code. The `StoreArt/KeyArt/` drop folder is ready if key art is generated later.

## Platform & tech

- **ASSUMPTION-03** three.js r186 (WebGL2), native ES modules with an import map, no bundler.
  Runs from any static server (`npm run serve`) and is laid out so the UWP kit can wrap `www/`.
- **ASSUMPTION-04** Target 60 fps on a mid-range PC GPU at 1080p; quality presets Low/Medium/High.
  `devicePixelRatio` capped at 1, render scale in Options.
- **ASSUMPTION-05** Collision uses `three-mesh-bvh` (MIT) — capsule vs triangle BVH.
- **ASSUMPTION-06** Saves go to `localStorage` (one profile, autosave after every stage).

## Core loop & mechanics (genre-faithful)

- **Ink is the resource, the score and the road.** Every surface is paintable unless marked
  otherwise. Hero ink (default tangerine) vs Murk ink (violet).
- **Kid form:** run 5.6 m/s, jump 1.6 m, shoots. Ink tank 100 units.
- **Squid form (hold Swim):** on your own ink the squid submerges — 10 m/s swim, invisible
  except for ripples, ink tank refills in ~2.6 s, health regenerates faster, you can **climb
  walls painted with your ink**, and jumping out is a higher "squid leap". Off your ink the squid
  just hops slowly and refills very slowly.
- **Enemy ink:** slows you to 35%, deals chip damage (never below 30 HP by itself), blocks swim
  and jump height.
- **Health:** 100 HP, regenerates after 1.2 s without damage. At 0 you "splat" and respawn at the
  last checkpoint (story) or base (turf).
- **Special gauge:** fills from the amount of turf you ink. When full, press Special.
- **Weapon kits** = main + sub + special, fixed per kit (8 kits, unlocked through the story).

volley), **Ink Jet** (jetpack flight with explosive shots).

## Modes

1. **Story Mode** — 4 worlds, 11 stages + 4 boss stages, cutscenes and radio dialogue.
2. **Turf Clash** — 4v4 turf war against bots on 3 arenas, 3 minutes, most ground inked wins,
   judged by the two otter judges. Unlocked from the start (the tutorial stage recommends it
   after World 1).
3. **Armory** — choose a kit, spend Pearls on upgrades (Ink Tank, Swim Speed, Armor, Special
   Charge).

## Story (full script lives in `docs/story.md`)

**Tidehaven** is a coastal city of Squidkin — kids who can shift between a kid form and a squid
form. The city's colour, and its ink, flow from the **Prism Wellspring** under the lighthouse.
**Baron Murkwell**, an anglerfish tycoon and CEO of **Murk Industries**, rips the five **Prism
Cores** out of the Wellspring to power his **Graytide Engine**, which will drown the city in
colourless Murk and let him sell colour back by the bottle. Rookie **Kai** is recruited by
**Commodore Brine** (a mantis-shrimp veteran of the old Splashguard) and guided over radio by
**Pix**, a jellyfish pirate-radio DJ and hacker, to win the cores back world by world.

| World | Setting | Stages | Boss |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Brinewater Docks | piers, containers, cranes, sunset | 1-1 First Splash · 1-2 Container Crossing · 1-3 Crane Climb | The Grinder (roller tank) |
| 2 Coral Heights | rooftops, skatepark, neon gardens, dusk | 2-1 Rooftop Rush · 2-2 Skatepark Sprawl · 2-3 Neon Gardens | The Bucketeer (flying bucket mech) |
| 3 Murk Refinery | pipes, vats, conveyors, toxic night | 3-1 Sludge Lines · 3-2 Pipe Dream · 3-3 Vat Valley | Sludge Serpent (eel mech) |
| 4 Murkwell Tower | the villain's spire in a storm | 4-1 The Ascent · 4-2 Gray Heart | Baron Murkwell — Graytide Mech |

Ending: the five cores return, the Wellspring floods the city with colour, credits roll over an
ink-painting montage; Turf Clash and all kits stay open for replay.

## Enemies (Murk Corps)

Gloopers (basic ink-blaster grunts), Shield Gloopers (front shield), Rollerbrutes (charging
rollers), Buzzdrones (flying bombers), Snipe Eels (charger snipers with a laser), Bomblobs
(bomb throwers), Murk Turrets (static) and Murk Pods (spawners). All of them paint Murk ink.

## Stage mechanics

Checkpoints, Prism Core goal capsule, Pearls (currency), a hidden Lost Postcard per stage (lore),
ink-activated sponges, squid launch pads, ink rails, shoot-to-open switches and gates, moving
platforms, Murk barriers, breakable crates, inflatable targets.

## Look

Bright, saturated, glossy. Chunky urban street-culture set dressing (graffiti, stickers, neon,
skate ramps), soft sunlight + hemisphere fill, bloom on ink highlights, wet reflective ink with a
raised, bumpy edge, stylised kids with tentacle hair in the ink colour. UI: left-rail menu over
a live 3D scene, Bungee display face, Baloo 2 UI face, tangerine/cyan accents on deep-sea navy
(`docs/concept/concept-1.png`, variant 1 chosen; variant 3 was rejected because its bottom bar
wraps "STORY MODE" onto two lines).

## Accessibility

Colour-blind ink palettes, invert X/Y, sensitivity sliders, aim assist toggle, camera shake
toggle, subtitle size, hold/toggle swim, full remapping, rumble toggle.

## Out of scope (stated honestly)

Online multiplayer, voice acting (dialogue uses synthesised gibberish "voice blips"), the MSIX
build itself (needs Windows + MSBuild).
